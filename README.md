# Grottle™ feedback

Public place for Grottle™ users to report bugs, suggest improvements, share gauge ideas, and share anonymous habit-shift readings.

## How it works
- In Grottle™, say **report a bug**, **suggest an idea**, or **share a gauge idea**. Grottle drafts the issue and gives you a prefilled link.
- Open the link, review the draft, and press **Submit** yourself. Grottle never posts for you.
- Grottle adds only your Grottle version, skin, and plan type. It leaves out your Sand %, bot names, and chat text unless you choose to keep them.
- Replies: we answer in the issue comments. GitHub notifies you, and you can reply right there. Ask Grottle **"any replies on my report?"** and it summarizes the public comments for you.

## Gauge ideas, math, and code
Ideas, formulas, sample code, and mockups are **proposals for review only**. Nothing from an issue is ever run or merged as-is. If an idea is accepted, it is rebuilt from scratch and must pass Grottle's golden visual checks before it ships.

## Habit-shift readings (info only)
When Grottle notices that one type of your work (for example Build) has used much more Sand per active hour than your usual rate, it may ask once whether you want to share an anonymous reading: habit, usual rate, recent rate, number of readings, date, and Grottle version. Several matching independent readings may lead the maintainer to post a watch note on grottle.app. Readings are information only. They make no claim about the cause.

## Privacy
Do not paste private data, tokens, or email addresses into issues. Issues are public.

## Labels
`bug`, `suggestion`, `gauge-idea`, `habit-shift`, `needs-triage`.
